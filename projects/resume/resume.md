Built the résumé in LaTeX using the developer-cv template. LaTeX is a natural fit for structured documents like CVs — consistent output, precise control over layout. It also came up as a learning project at the time, since it's common enough in technical and academic contexts that it seemed worth getting familiar with.

The résumé itself is available as a PDF download on the career page — this is just the source. The project is done, but the document is a living thing. As of writing this, it's a couple of years out of date and overdue an update.
